## About the film

The rain stops. The power goes out. For a few minutes, the city has to look at itself without the usual glow.

After the Rain is a live-action study in atmosphere, memory, and the things that become visible when the familiar systems go quiet.

## Working note

This film is being developed as a compact, location-led piece: a small crew, practical light, and a camera willing to wait.
