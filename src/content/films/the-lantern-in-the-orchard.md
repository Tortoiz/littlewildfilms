## About the film

The orchard has always known when spring is coming. This year, it forgets.

A child carrying a paper lantern walks between the trees, looking for the one blossom that still remembers the season. Along the way, the orchard begins to return small pieces of the past: a lost ribbon, a song from an empty house, the sound of rain on a roof that no longer exists.

## Director’s note

I wanted to make a film about wonder without making the world feel weightless. The visual language begins with hand-painted animation, quiet landscapes, and the warmth of a small light against a large night.

The film is interested in the spaces between adventure and stillness — the moment before a door opens, the path after everyone else has gone home, and the feeling that a place might be waiting for you.
