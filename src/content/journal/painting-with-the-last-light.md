Blue hour is a narrow door.

It does not stay open long enough for certainty, which is exactly why it felt right for this film.

## Looking for the in-between

We kept returning to photographs where the sky was not quite blue and the ground was not quite dark. The image had to feel like it was changing while we looked at it.

For the film, that became a rule: every frame should carry a little of the previous light and a little of the next darkness.
