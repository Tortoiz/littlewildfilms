# Little Wild Films — Brand Spec

## Design read

Little Wild Films is positioned as an independent production house for animated, live-action, and AI-assisted cinema. The v0 uses a kinetic editorial film-studio language: cinematic, sparse, emotionally grounded, and visibly authored rather than tool-led.

## Type

- Display: Instrument Serif
- Body: DM Sans
- Metadata: DM Mono

## Layout and motion

- 8px spacing base with large 10–15vh section spacing.
- Mostly sharp corners; no generic SaaS card language.
- Slow image drift, staggered entry, restrained hover transitions.
- Reduced-motion mode removes non-essential animation.

## Content assumptions in v0

The film titles “Mosslight” and “After the Rain” are working titles used to establish the content architecture. Replace them with real films, stills, and descriptions when available.

## Voice

Cinematic, clear, poetic without becoming vague. AI is framed as part of the process, never the product itself.
