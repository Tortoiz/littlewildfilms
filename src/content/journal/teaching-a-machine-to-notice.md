The useful prompt is often the one that fails beautifully.

For Mosslight, we are less interested in asking a machine to finish a forest than in asking it to show us a forest we would not have thought to draw.

## Keep the question alive

The job of direction is to decide what to keep, what to reject, and what new question an image creates. The tool can provide momentum, but it cannot decide what the film means.

That part still belongs to attention.
