Weather changes the rhythm of a scene before anyone speaks.

In After the Rain, the storm is not an event we cut away from. It is the thing that makes the city briefly unfamiliar.

## Practical atmosphere

The production question is simple but difficult: how do we let the weather shape the performance without asking the weather to perform on cue?

The answer so far is patience. We are building the schedule around the sky instead of asking the sky to fit inside the schedule.
